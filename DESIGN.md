## Overview

The page is a lobby directory. Timber is the building. Ivory plates are the cards you can read. Brass marks the action and the rail. Real screenshots sit in recessed frames. The first frame is a booking site, with the offer on a card pulled forward.

## Colors

Timber (#1c1612) is the page. Ivory (#f4efe6) is every long read: about, price, questions, and the offer card. Brass (#c6a060) is the contact button, directory links, and the rail. Ink (#241c16) is text on ivory. Muted (#5c5148) is secondary text on ivory and clears WCAG AA. Do not put gray text on brass or timber.

## Typography

Bodoni Moda for the offer, section titles, and project names. Schibsted Grotesk for everything you read. Body measure stays near 68 characters. No eyebrow labels above headings.

## Layout

One column of plates on timber, max width 72rem. The first viewport is a full-width frame. On wide screens the offer card sits on the lower left of that frame. The directory hangs off a 1px brass rail. Wide entries span the rail. Narrow entries sit two-up from 800px.

## Elevation & Depth

Frames use a soft offset shadow (0 16px 36px, dark brown at low opacity). The offer card uses a similar shadow so it sits in front of the photograph. No hard offset shadows.

## Shapes

Corners are square. Frames have a timber bezel of about 8px. Screenshots are cropped from the top so the product's own header stays in view.

## Components

The primary action is a brass rectangle labeled Contact. It sends email. Directory links are brass and underlined. Questions use a native disclosure. Focus is a 2px brass ring.

## Do's and Don'ts

Do show a real site before the price. Do keep the rate card lines exact. Do not add eyebrows, section numbers, icon tiles, or equal cards of icon plus heading plus text. Do not use cream-and-serif as the whole page, or a navy services header. Motion is one short reveal on the first photograph, and it is off when the visitor asks for less motion.
